🍎 Ontario Curriculum Connection — Grade 2 Science

Strand B: Structures and Mechanisms — Movement and Simple Machines

This web application aligns directly with Ontario Grade 2 Science and Technology expectations. It provides a non-punitive, multi-sensory, and accessible pathway for all students, including neurodiverse learners and students with special education needs.

Curriculum Expectations Addressed:

  • B1. Relating Science and Technology to Our World: Identify simple machines in everyday life (e.g. ramps, scissors, doorstops, wheelbarrows, flagpoles) and assess how they make tasks easier.
  • B2. Developing Investigation and Communication Skills: Investigate how simple machines move objects and change the direction or amount of force needed.
  • B3. Understanding Basic Concepts: Identify six simple machines: lever, wheel and axle, pulley, inclined plane, wedge, and screw; describe their basic purpose and functions.

Level Progression Alignment:

Level 1 (Meet Machines): Visual recognition & basic concepts (B3.1).
Level 2 & 3 (Matching & Names): Visual discrimination & vocabulary building.
Level 4 & 5 (Everyday Objects): Connecting concepts to real-world objects (B1.1).
Level 6 & 7 (Purposes & Problem Solving): Understanding mechanical function & application (B2.2).
Level 8 & 9 (Sorting & Comparing): Higher-order categorisation and decision-making (B3.2).
Level 10 (Challenge): Comprehensive formative assessment.

Special Education & Universal Design for Learning (UDL) Features:

• Strict Non-Punitive Rule: Choosing an incorrect option results in silence and no state shift—preventing anxiety and encouraging safe retry.
• Integrated TTS & Audio: All text can be read aloud using Web Speech API.
• SVG Visual System: Crisp vector illustrations instead of external image dependencies.
• Touch-Optimised: Target sizes exceeding 60px for Chromebook touchscreens and iPads.
